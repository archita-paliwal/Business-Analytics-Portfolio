# Insurance Premium Prediction

## Business Problem

Insurance companies need to estimate premium amounts accurately to balance customer affordability with business profitability. Incorrect premium estimation can lead to financial losses or reduced customer acquisition.

This project develops a regression model to identify the factors that significantly influence insurance premium pricing.

---

## Objective

To predict insurance premium amounts using customer demographic, lifestyle, and health-related attributes.

---

## Dataset

- Insurance Customer Dataset
- Multiple customer and policy attributes

---

## Methodology

- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Multiple Linear Regression
- Feature Significance Analysis
- Model Evaluation

---

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Scikit-learn
- Matplotlib
- Excel

---

## Key Findings

- Age and smoking status were among the strongest drivers of insurance premiums.
- BMI and medical risk factors showed a positive relationship with premium amounts.
- The regression model effectively explained premium variation across customers.

---

## Business Impact

The model provides a transparent, data-driven approach to premium estimation, helping insurers improve pricing consistency, assess customer risk more effectively, and support informed underwriting decisions.
