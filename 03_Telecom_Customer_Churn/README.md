# Telecom Customer Churn Prediction

## Business Problem

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for telecom companies. Losing existing customers increases acquisition costs and reduces long-term profitability.

This project identifies which customer segments are most likely to churn and estimates customer survival time to support proactive retention strategies.

---

## Objective

To analyze customer retention patterns and identify the factors influencing churn using survival analysis.

---

## Dataset

- 1,000 Telecom Customers

---

## Methodology

- Kaplan-Meier Survival Analysis
- Log-Rank Test
- Breslow Test
- Tarone-Ware Test

---

## Tools Used

- SPSS
- Microsoft Excel

---

## Key Findings

- Basic-tier customers churn nearly three times faster than Premium-tier customers.
- Median customer survival:
  - Basic Plan: ~22 months
  - Premium Plan: ~69 months
- Early retention interventions significantly improve customer lifetime value.

---

## Business Impact

Telecom companies should prioritize retention campaigns for Basic-plan customers before the 20-month mark instead of applying uniform retention strategies across all customer segments.
