# Credit Risk Assessment

## Business Problem

Banks must identify high-risk borrowers before approving loans. Incorrect lending decisions increase default risk and financial losses.

This project develops a logistic regression model to identify the factors that significantly influence loan default.

---

## Objective

To predict loan default probability using borrower characteristics.

---

## Dataset

- 500 Loan Applicants

---

## Methodology

- Logistic Regression
- Model Evaluation
- Statistical Significance Testing

---

## Tools Used

- SPSS
- Microsoft Excel

---

## Key Findings

- Employment tenure and existing credit card debt were the strongest predictors of loan default.
- Demographic variables such as age, education and income were not statistically significant.

---

## Business Impact

Financial institutions should prioritize behavioural credit indicators over demographic characteristics while evaluating loan applications.
