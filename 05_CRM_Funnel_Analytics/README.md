# CRM Funnel Analytics

## Business Problem

Education businesses often generate a high volume of leads but struggle to convert them into registrations due to delays in follow-ups, inconsistent CRM tracking, and funnel leakages.

This project analyzes the admissions funnel to identify conversion bottlenecks and recommend improvements to increase registrations.

---

## Objective

To identify where leads drop across the admissions funnel and improve conversion through CRM analytics.

---

## Dataset

- CRM Lead Data
- Google Ads
- Meta Ads
- Registration Data

---

## Methodology

- Funnel Analysis
- Conversion Analysis
- KPI Tracking
- Root Cause Analysis
- Dashboard Reporting

---

## Tools Used

- Excel
- Power BI
- SQL

---

## Key Findings

- Google Ads generated significantly higher-quality leads than Meta.
- Delayed first contact reduced lead conversion.
- Missed follow-ups created avoidable funnel leakage.
- CRM SLA improvements strengthened lead management.

---

## Business Impact

The analysis helped identify operational improvements in lead management, enabling faster follow-ups, better campaign evaluation, and more efficient allocation of marketing budgets.
